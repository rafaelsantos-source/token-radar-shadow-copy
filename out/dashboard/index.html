How the copy score works

Five signals from the snapshot alone, each 0 to 1: inflow (7-day TVL flow, 30%), scale (log TVL, 20%), breadth (chain count, 15%), persistence (age, 15%), category heat (the median 7-day flow of the category's pre-token peers, 20%). Implied value = TVL × 0.22 × (0.35 + 1.6 × copy score). Tier A mirrors $250, tier B $150, tier C $75 — sizing, not exclusion: the board carries every rankable pre-token protocol, watchlist and early alike.
